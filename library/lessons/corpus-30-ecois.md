## How to adapt the lesson

1. Identify the equivalent subject in the current essay: a person, material, obligation, decision or transaction. Keep that subject visible across the passage.
2. Name the problem that the proposed arrangement changes. Explain the causal link before naming a framework or component.
3. Write the current project’s own sequence in ordinary prose. Use the sequence above as an analytical pattern; preserve neither the source’s domain-specific claims nor its nouns by simple substitution.
4. Add an exhibit only when it answers a particular reader question. The reader must still understand the core mechanism with the appendix closed.
5. Check the current competition guidebook and current authorial brief. This source’s length, typography, claims of success and administrative apparatus are not controlling rules.
