## The useful argumentative move

**Whole-system picture**

Use when an ambitious engineered arrangement is hard to picture. Explain the object, input/output and operating sequence before a subsystem catalogue. A future partner table should give each actor a job rather than decorate a diagram with prestigious names.

The transferable sequence is:

**Scope the proposal, show the complete arrangement, explain the stages and then name the enabling components.**
