## Controlling order

These sources were supplied before or during JejakBalik. The prior project read their available text completely and inspected their analytical/visual material. Selected pages were opened again while building the reference atlas. This guide identifies useful design and writing patterns; it is not a new independent validation of every external claim inside the source PDFs.

Use the current author’s explicit instructions, the current competition’s rules and V5 to resolve a conflict. An older essay is a model for a particular move. Its page count, target market, palette, sample, numbers and administrative identities are not settings to inherit.

The originals are preserved under `library/originals/`; exact byte hashes, original upload names and persistent identities are in `library/source-manifest.json`. The two business-plan PDFs are not generic templates for making a short innovation essay into a pitch deck.

## ReFeel

**Available source:** 55 PDF pages; original filename `Semifinal Round_Faga_Hamiz Ghanir_BPC21MX..pdf`.

**Best jobs:** analytical chart design; service explanation through a purchase decision; paired participant value; linking a body result to inspectable assumptions and calculations; prototype/campaign concept imagery alongside analytical figures.

Useful current-page anchors:

| Physical page | Why open it |
|---|---|
| 4–5 | Background into the information difficulty and its supporting analysis. |
| 7 | Merchant and buyer value, with jobs/gains/pains organized around the service. |
| 8 | Revenue layers and who receives the value they pay for. |
| 12–13 | Initial market segment, alternatives and market-scope graphic. |
| 31 | Nested TAM/SAM/SOM schematic plus the actual calculation table and source/assumption language. |
| 40 | Detailed service workflow and the place of existing sales channels. |
| 43–45 | Financial schedules, contribution economics and cash/reserve interpretation. |
| 50 | Intended outcomes linked to measures and interpretation limits. |

The nested market exhibit works because each level has a visible label, number, explanation and leader line. The source expressly says the circles are schematic. Do not calculate radii as if their area represented the money unless the chart explicitly uses that encoding. Do not replace service revenue with merchandise value, annual orders with cumulative users, or a sourced count with an unmarked team assumption.

The small purchase-decision strip supplied separately is especially useful: it locates the service at a specific decision rather than trying to explain the entire company in one illustration. The value-proposition diagram makes the two sides legible through their jobs and difficulties. In a short essay, the body still needs to explain how those sides meet.

The source’s decorative background, large repeated branding and colored headings are business-plan choices. For a competition requiring black text, transfer the geometry and hierarchy while using the required document treatment. Use native DOCX tables for schedules/matrices whenever they remain readable and editable. The market and complex flow layouts are good candidates for TikZ/PGFPlots.

## Dua

**Available source:** 58 PDF pages; original filename `Dua_Athifa Ardhani_UI.pdf`.

**Best jobs:** product/usage transitions; clear conceptual mockups with dimensions and parts; source-aware comparisons; implementation through owners, dates, indicators and responses; compact quantitative appendices.

Useful current-page anchors:

| Physical page | Why open it |
|---|---|
| 3–5 | Daily use and the proposed modular arrangement before the complete product catalogue. |
| 6–7 | Product dimensions, modular relationship and product-studio presentation. |
| 43 | Material/part specifications paired with the inspection each requires. |
| 44–46 | Actual supplied studio screenshots and the relationship between dimensions, internals and connection details. |
| 47 | Market calculation inputs, team assumptions and yearly unit basis. |
| 48 | Competitor products plus weighted feature/evidence table. |
| 49 | Weighted industry forces with a stated scoring scale. |
| 53–54 | Production/QC stages, facility capacity and responsibility. |
| 55–57 | Unit costs, funding, forecasts and scenario interpretation. |
| 58 | Gantt, indicators, risk triggers and responses in one coherent implementation appendix. |

The competitor table does not automatically give the proposed product the highest overall score. The displayed own-product evidence/quality score is low, and its total is below one comparator. That is a useful design principle: preserve the meaning of the scoring basis instead of using a weighted matrix as decoration. The numbers and source prices remain Dua’s historical reference values and must be researched anew for another market.

The last-page implementation exhibit is a strong template for the *relationship* between schedule, owner, milestone, operational indicator and decision. Do not copy its years, cities, buffer or launch sequence. A different service may need a short commissioning Gantt plus a longer strategic horizon, as JejakBalik did.

Mockups can make a product comprehensible, especially when they show how parts fit. They do not establish manufacturing, usability, customer acceptance or a deployed studio merely because they look finished. Keep a conceptual render distinguishable from an actual screenshot or prototype capture.

## SIRKULA

**Available source:** 94 PDF pages; original long upload name is preserved in the manifest; the portable original path is `library/originals/essays/SIRKULA.pdf`.

**Best jobs:** turning a circular-economy proposal into an operating arrangement; separating participant ownership, service payments and material/product flows; sustained implementation and explicit resources; a detailed dossier attached to an intelligible main argument.

Useful anchors include physical pp.3–4 for the abstract/opening, pp.11–12 for participant roles and material/payment flow, p.17 for finance/cash interpretation, pp.55 onward for participant value and strategic priorities, around p.63 for commissioning reviews, and pp.83–87 for funded operating safeguards and response ownership. These locators are reading leads; inspect the complete selected source before quoting it or making a new claim about it.

The durable lesson is to follow one material or obligation into the work, participant incentive, funds and destination that make it usable. Ownership, sales proceeds and coordinator income should not be collapsed into one revenue claim. A household-owned asset can have a different payer and a different party coordinating the service.

The 94-page length is not a quality target. It demonstrates why a large dossier needs an index, exact navigation and an exhibit-selection discipline. New work should remove duplicated mappings and self-description rather than treating available appendix space as a reason to add prose.

## Gotong Sampah

**Available source:** 33 PDF pages; portable original path `library/originals/essays/Gotong-Sampah.pdf`.

**Best jobs:** connecting an environmental flow with the participants doing the work and the distribution of economic value; defining a social-enterprise arrangement beyond charitable messaging; implementation phases that continue after initial activity.

The opening, beginning on physical p.3, centers the workers carrying the waste-management burden. Later sections connect the operating/business arrangement and implementation, including a phased discussion around physical p.11. This is useful when a new proposal describes environmental benefit but does not explain the people, paid work and rights that sustain it.

Transfer the relationship between value creation and the participant’s role. Do not transfer asserted partner access, revenue numbers, scale, labor outcomes or a “formalized profession” result without support in the new project. A social contribution should be defined in measurable terms suited to the claim: paid work-hours are not automatically new jobs, devices received are not recovered material, and a target is not an achieved result.

## ReGenCity Grid Indonesia

**Available source:** the supplied DOCX template reference, preserved under `library/originals/templates/` with its original filename in the manifest.

**Best jobs:** Word document typography and appendix treatment; an audience-facing essay with a supporting dossier; consistent numbering, tables, captions and cross-references.

Use the document as a visual/layout reference under the new guidebook. Build the new file with cleared body content, current cover/header decisions, current organizer logos and neutral author metadata. Never carry over participant names, student numbers, declarations, old hyperlinks, unused custom XML, hidden comments or tracked changes.

JejakBalik adopted Times New Roman, black text, native tables and its appendix treatment, then changed paragraph spacing/indentation, clickable navigation and continuous appendix packing in response to the user. Those later corrections outrank the inherited template. Its exact competition structure, brand, energy topic and author identities are not generic defaults.

## PI MT writing sample

**Meaningful title:** *Urgency, or the Appearance of Urgency? A Corpus-Assisted Critical Discourse Analysis of Institutional Legitimation in UN SDG 13 Discourse*.

**Available source:** nine PDF pages; original filename `PI MT.docx.pdf`.

**Best job:** authorial cadence and paragraph continuity in English. The user supplied this specifically because the prose develops relationships with transitions rather than ending every paragraph in an isolated verdict.

The opening moves from SDG discourse to the language of urgency, then to institutional responsibility and the research question. Phrases such as “In doing this” and “At the same time” work because they advance that relationship. They are not a checklist of connectors to insert automatically. The subject of the preceding sentence gives the next sentence something to develop.

Read for these behaviors:

1. A familiar subject narrows toward a specific interpretive question.
2. An example explains the relation being examined rather than interrupting it.
3. The next paragraph takes up a consequence of the prior paragraph.
4. Source discussion leads into the writer’s chosen analytical purpose.
5. Qualifications distinguish what language shows from what institutions have actually done.

It is a research writing sample, not the mandatory genre of an innovation proposal. Its research question/method/literature apparatus does not replace a developed-system explanation, institutional incentives, implementation or a defensible recommendation. V5 controls confident innovation advocacy, while the writing sample helps achieve continuity and precision.

## How to combine the models

For a short innovation essay, combine a background that narrows to a specific problem, a clear definition of the intended arrangement, one representative operating case and a credible implementation judgment. Use the appropriate model for each job. Keep the new essay’s central argument visible with its appendices closed.

For the supporting dossier, use the restrained analytical geometry of ReFeel/Dua, the editable document discipline of ReGenCity and the operating accountability seen in SIRKULA. Apply the user’s later preferences: black text, limited palette, native tables first, readable precise diagrams, compact continuous flow, sentence-level appendix calls and explicit return links. Keep the deeper source files for examination; they do not all have to be loaded or copied into each new essay.
