## The useful argumentative move

**Norm to practice gap**

A normative standard matters when the reader sees the practice that falls short, the insufficiency of an existing alternative and the institutional response.

The transferable sequence is:

**Norm → practice gap → existing alternative → institution and response.**

Use this reference when the draft has this symptom: **Law or policy descriptions occupy the opening without identifying a concrete failure of practice.**
