## A visually persuasive source can still have wrong arithmetic

Preview 09 displays weights of **0.20, 0.30, 0.10, 0.15, 0.15 and 0.15**. They total **1.05**, not 1.00. The own-brand Incentive Profit row displays rating **4**, weight **0.15**, and score **0.45**; their product is **0.60**. The screenshot is preserved as supplied. It is a layout reference, not a scoring workbook to reuse.

For a new weighted matrix:

1. Define factors and scale before scoring alternatives.
2. Make weights sum to 1.00 and identify whose judgment they represent.
3. Compute each score as weight × rating and sum exactly.
4. Retain the stated denominator/scale and handle unknown evidence openly.
5. Explain what changes if a plausible weight or rating changes.
6. Keep price separate when its relationship to the scoring scale is different.

Dua physical p.48 provides a useful contrasting pattern: the proposed product is not assigned the highest score on every factor or the highest weighted total. The source’s own figure and historical evidence remain its own; the lesson is fair, defined comparison.

## A beautiful issue tree still needs a valid causal argument

The desired geometry is goal/problem → multiple subproblems → specific issues/root causes. Each leaf should identify a cause that the mechanism addresses. Attach a quantitative finding or a credible citation at the appropriate scope. Do not invent a local percentage because a global or regional study exists. Do not put design constraints, legal duties and symptoms under the label “measured root causes” without explaining the inference.

Use native Word tables first if a simple hierarchy remains clear. Use TikZ when branching, merged levels, connectors or exact alignment make native layout awkward. A diagram must remain readable at its actual width in the document. Long Indonesian labels require a deliberate line break/column width, not automatic font reduction.

## Analytic graphics, concepts and screenshots have different jobs

- **Precise chart or table:** calculate from actual inputs and render with native tables, TikZ/PGFPlots or an appropriate plotting tool. Generative image text is not a reliable numerical chart.
- **Concept/mockup:** use GPT Image when available and helpful. Label the depicted arrangement in a meaningful way, such as “Rancangan konsep”, without pretending it is an operating facility or measured outcome.
- **Actual screenshot:** capture or use the supplied real interface state. Crop only to improve composition/readability while preserving values and state. Keep a manifest of the source frame, crop and meaning.

The JejakBalik city/facility/device strip uses owner-provided 3D interface frames. The facility is an intended arrangement visualized in a prototype, not an existing partner warehouse. The laptop is not evidence of repair or sanitization. The current 18-request local planner and older 48-device public demo remain distinct in the case record.

## Restrained adaptation

The user likes authored analytical exhibits integrated into a document, with captions and evidence where needed. This does not require slide-like titles, decorative banners, checkerboard fills, dramatic gradients or a box explaining the “key takeaway”. Use the current competition’s fonts and colors. Keep ordinary table rows white, align labels and numbers meaningfully, and preserve space for reading rather than forcing every visual into the same card layout.
