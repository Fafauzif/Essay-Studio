## Recoverability and provenance

The source-recovery manifest records byte sizes, SHA-256 hashes, original names, persistent identities and local paths. An original filename is retained for preservation; cite its meaningful title or description in audience-facing work. Keep this complete archive private. Do not copy cover names, student numbers, declarations or signatures into a new document.
