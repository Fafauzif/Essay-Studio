## Receipt carried from the JejakBalik project

The prior essay team read **all available substantive contents** of the supplied reference corpus, including references, appendices, relevant covers/declarations and image-only content. It used native extraction, bounded sequential reads, OCR for five image-only PDFs and visual inspection. This package preserves that prior reading record. During repository assembly selected pages were inspected again for preview selection; assembling the archive is not described as a new independent full reading of every document.

The reference ZIP contains 37 entries, representing **35 hash-unique files**: 30 unique PDFs totaling **789 pages present**, plus five DOCX files. The native extraction counted 125,395 words; that excludes OCR-only text and is not a claim that only those words were read. Exact duplicates: ID23 = ID17 (Curhatin Yuk) and ID33 = ID10 (E-CENG). The untouched original ZIP and all duplicate member files remain in the private archive.

This is an available-material reading receipt, not independent validation of every reference’s scientific, legal, market or financial assertion. The name of a file may describe a competition result; that result was not independently certified during this work.

## Do not repair source limits by fabrication

A catalogue may preserve an incomplete source. A new essay may use its readable argument pattern. Neither act makes the missing pages, blurred code, unknown test procedure or absent mockup available. Keep the distinction between a useful writing model and a verified evidence source. The new essay’s own factual claims need primary sources suited to their scope.
