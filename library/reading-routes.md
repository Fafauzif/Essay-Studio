## Start with the actual task

Read the current competition guidebook, current brief, author’s current draft/comments and newly supplied project documents completely before substantive work. Read the controlling V5 manual fully when beginning work under it; do not claim a full read from this catalog or an earlier assistant’s receipt. Record unavailable/truncated material precisely and obtain what is needed, subject to the explicit availability decisions already made for the historical archive.

The reference library is indexed so a new task can select relevant examples without loading every historical file into the model at once. Select a small set for a clear reason; read each selected document completely before using it to substantiate a new claim about its contents. The available-material receipts preserved here support choosing what to open. They do not give a new assistant permission to claim an independent full reading that did not happen.

Do not load the enormous JejakBalik master record as the mandatory starting prompt for unrelated essays. Its product-specific facts and numerical assumptions are a case study. Read that case when reproducing, revising or learning the end-to-end workflow, not as a source of universal competition rules.

## Choose references by the draft’s problem

| Draft problem | Primary route | What to look for | What the next draft must do |
|---|---|---|---|
| The opening names the product before establishing urgency | [FISHEE.ID](lessons/corpus-04-fishee-id.md), then [Jakarta Smart Island](lessons/corpus-08-jakarta-smart-island.md) | Narrowing from sector/problem to a specific arrangement gap | Establish the unresolved problem before the adopted solution, within the current page budget. |
| The idea sounds like separate app features | [Integrasi Tiga Titik](lessons/corpus-09-integrasi-tiga-titik.md), [SAKARA](lessons/corpus-37-sakara.md), SIM-Sehat in [the anthology](lessons/corpus-20-essay-anthology-excerpt.md) | One transaction, material or obligation passing through actors | Explain the intended service as a continuous case with decisions and resources. |
| The technology is tangible but operations are vague | [Jakarta Smart Island](lessons/corpus-08-jakarta-smart-island.md), [POSEIDON](lessons/corpus-29-poseidon.md) | Flow into capacity, work, resources and actor/date phases | Show who does what, with what capacity/funding, and what happens next. |
| The mechanism jumps from input to outcome | [ADRODECO](lessons/corpus-24-adrodeco.md), [SOLVAEROPHIL](lessons/corpus-11-solvaerophil.md), [Cinnamaldehyde](lessons/corpus-27-cinnamaldehyde-and-type-a-polyphenols.md) | Intermediate condition, parameter or action | Connect each decisive link with a reason and appropriately scoped support. |
| “Stakeholders” is only a list | Eventlink and Citizen Charter in [Kumpulan Naskah Esai](lessons/corpus-32-kumpulan-naskah-esai.md) | Availability, selection, response, time/cost/responsibility | Give each actor a concrete decision, incentive, task and continuing obligation. |
| Prose has uniform paragraph weight and canned conclusions | [Nostalgia](lessons/corpus-36-nostalgia.md), [The Nexus Point](lessons/corpus-28-nexus-point.md), the [PI MT writing sample](lessons/user-models.md) | A subject or question evolving across paragraphs | Preserve authorial judgment and transitions that explain a relationship. |
| The appendix is a second repetitive essay | [ReFeel/Dua/ReGenCity models](lessons/user-models.md), then [the approved JejakBalik case](../cases/jejakbalik/README.md) | An exhibit’s specific job and its inline claim | Compress derivations and mappings into readable exhibits with exact navigation. |
| A model or survey is being added for prestige | [Jenis-Jenis Penelitian](lessons/corpus-06-jenis-jenis-penelitian.md), plus V5 | What question the evidence can answer | Select a method only if its output changes an implementation or argument decision. |
| The local pilot is disconnected from the promised final service | [AGRIVERSE](lessons/corpus-22-agriverse.md), [Curhatin Yuk](lessons/corpus-17-curhatin-yuk.md) | A local operating configuration and continuing actor/resource work | Explain what repeats in another area and what each area must supply. |
| A conclusion repeats every component | [Nostalgia](lessons/corpus-36-nostalgia.md), [SAKARA](lessons/corpus-37-sakara.md) with their limits | Synthesis of the consequence of the arrangement | Close the actual argument without new major claims or a final operating checklist. |

A source can be useful for one move and weak for another. The catalog records genre: a research article, PKM report or teaching deck does not become the default format for a short competition essay.

## Use style without borrowing evidence

Keep three uses separate:

| Use | Permitted transfer | What needs fresh support |
|---|---|---|
| Argument pattern | How an opening narrows, how a mechanism is explained, how a paragraph advances | Any factual or causal claim in the new topic |
| Exhibit design | Hierarchy, alignment, whitespace, comparable axes, navigation | Every new value, population, unit and scenario boundary |
| Domain evidence | A lead to an underlying paper/report | Read and verify the actual primary source, publication details and exact claim scope |

A source essay’s respondents, survey percentages, prototype tests, partners, financial forecasts and claims of success remain that source’s claims. Do not copy them into a new proposal, attribute them to the new team or infer that a prize-like filename proves quality. A source can demonstrate an effective way to explain a study even when its study itself has not been independently verified here.

When adapting a good figure, recreate the analytical structure from the new project’s real data and assumptions. Do not paste a competitor’s logo into a higher quadrant, give the new team maximum scores automatically, or invent percentages to fill a symmetrical tree.

## Read an example in three passes

1. **Argument pass.** Write a one-sentence account of what the author asks the reader to believe and what chain makes it plausible. Identify each paragraph’s job. Check whether the same subject/action/obligation continues between paragraphs.
2. **Operating pass.** Sketch the actual flow in ordinary language: participants, input, decision, output, resources and unresolved cases. A list of modules is not a flow.
3. **Exhibit pass.** For each figure/table used as a model, name its reader question, data/input, unit, scope and link to a sentence. Observe its actual placed size and caption. Identify where tiny labels or duplicate panels reduce value.

The resulting note should be specific: “the next paragraph follows who repays the funding” is useful; “good flow and strong innovation” is not. Do not turn the reading note itself into the new essay.

## English and Indonesian

Use the source language to learn its reasoning and pacing. Write the new essay in the requested language and follow the current guidebook. A natural Indonesian transition may need a different sentence structure in English; literal translation of every connector can create repetitive prose.

Useful transfer questions in both languages:

- **Indonesia:** Apa yang berubah pada akhir paragraf ini, dan mengapa paragraf berikutnya dibutuhkan?
- **English:** What has changed in the reader’s understanding, and why does the next paragraph follow?
- **Indonesia:** Siapa yang mengambil keputusan dan menanggung pekerjaan berikutnya?
- **English:** Who makes the decision and carries the next obligation?
- **Indonesia:** Angka ini menunjukkan apa, pada populasi dan periode mana?
- **English:** What does this number measure, for which population and period?

## When a source is incomplete

Use the exact [availability ledger](source-availability.md). Missing material cannot be inferred from a heading, a thumbnail, a duplicate or a similar essay. The historical user explicitly could not supply the missing originals and authorized work with available information. Do not repeatedly ask for the same GINTECH or anthology continuation. If a new task depends on a genuinely unavailable passage, identify that exact dependency rather than declaring the entire library unusable.
