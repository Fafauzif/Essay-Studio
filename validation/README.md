## Boundaries

Historical source facts were not all re-researched merely to package their
unchanged originals. Earlier verified reading lessons and exact source limits
are attributed accurately. Scripts extract or render material; that alone
does not prove an assistant read it or verify a research claim. The document
auditor checks structure and stated formatting, not whether a cited source
supports the claim or a business model is credible.

The original annotated-DOCX binary was not exposed by the current read-only
recovery route. All 49 original comment quotes, selected scope, requested
actions and closure evidence are preserved in the master/case comment ledger.
GINTECH missing printed pages 8–10 and the cut-off anthology continuation remain
original-source gaps; no missing text was reconstructed as fact.

LaTeX binary presence is not a compile test. This environment's LuaLaTeX lacked
required modules; functional XeLaTeX was used. Dense examples should generally
be placed around 16 cm wide and reflowed/split when needed; nominal font settings
do not guarantee effective type size after resizing. Every future final essay
still needs its own page-by-page rendered review and guidebook checks.

No personal skill installation, repository publication, partner contact or
competition submission was performed. Review scores are calibrated judgments
within each declared scope. All final applicable dimensions met 8.5 after
concrete repairs; no score was raised solely to pass the threshold.
