# Essay Studio style map

The author's preferences combine connected argumentative prose with restrained analytical exhibits. Read [Writing and voice](../skills/write-innovation-essays/references/writing-and-voice.md) for the full editorial guidance and honestly constructed Indonesian/English examples.

## Prose

Establish the problem and relevant context before deep proposal detail. Introduce the intended arrangement plainly at the agreed point. Explain actors, work, resources, decisions and benefit before brand labels. Let each paragraph develop the preceding one; avoid generic era openings, repetitive transition words, uniform paragraph weight and conspicuous final-sounding recaps.

Write confidently about the proposed design. Keep facts, plans, estimates, targets, illustrations and observed achievements accurate. Place a decisive qualification where it changes interpretation and use targeted implementation responses rather than repeated validation warnings.

## Exhibits

Use native Word tables first. Use TikZ/PGFPlots for exact diagrams and suitable numerical charts. Use actual captures for interface evidence and conceptual imagery for a clearly conceptual job. Keep sources, values and transformations traceable.

Prefer clear hierarchy, consistent type, black text, restrained color and white table body rows. Avoid slide-name banners, ornamental cards, alternating checkerboard fills, heavy borders and self-explanatory AI-style callouts. The current guidebook overrides house defaults.

Read [Visuals and imagery](../skills/write-innovation-essays/references/visual-and-imagery.md) and [Appendices and navigation](../skills/write-innovation-essays/references/appendices-and-navigation.md) before building the dossier. Check actual placed size: resolution alone does not make a small screenshot readable.

## Evidence presentation

Keep headline results and their meaning in the essay. Place visible appendix links beside the supported claims, with an individually linked index and explicit returns. Minimize appendix prose to necessary captions, source/assumption notes, legends and interpretation. Let appendices flow continuously without forcing every subsection onto a new page.

Use meaningful source identities and real bibliography details. Upload filenames, tool identifiers, local paths, internal cells and production commentary belong in working records rather than audience citations.

## Reference use

ReFeel/Dua illustrate analytical chart taste; ReGenCity illustrates document/appendix organization; supplied essays illustrate actual argumentative movement. Their weaknesses and mismatched template content remain excluded. V5 and the author's current instructions take precedence over earlier examples.
