## The case’s central arrangement

JejakBalik coordinates electronics returns by matching requests with capacity on journeys already planned by several logistics providers. Institutions pay for recurring coordination and agreed transport/receiving work. Accepted receivers and accountable handoffs keep the device, current holder, decisions and unresolved obligations connected.

The three mechanisms support each other. Capacity becomes usable when it meets timing, category, owner instructions and receiving conditions. Institutional payment funds work even when an item has no resale proceeds. Handoff records and case ownership preserve responsibility through cancellation, data holds and changed receiving conditions.

The adopted first area is UI/Depok; the intended system extends through institution programmes and region-specific provider/receiver relationships. Campus facilities, opang and other named organizations were prospective arrangements, not signed resources. No partner contact, public deployment or competition submission is authorized by this archive.

## Why the revision mattered

The rejected earlier approach introduced the solution too early and sounded like a prototype-development report. The final approach established the problem first, defined JejakBalik plainly in Gagasan, used a three-scene overview and explained a continuous return case. It made the institutional purchase and renewal logic clearer and carried the argument with the appendices closed.

The dossier changed from long explanatory prose and scattered navigation to decision-bearing exhibits. It uses native tables where appropriate, exact sentence-level calls, individually linked subsections and visible returns. The first appendix contains access QR. The issue tree is horizontal. The implementation includes finances, two Gantts, roles and funded risks. Each detailed exhibit has a job rather than merely demonstrating that a framework exists.

The 49 comments are retained in `source-current/all-49-comments.json` and verbatim with scopes/actions in V4. The original annotated DOCX binary could not be recovered from the now-replaced current file identity; the full feedback content remains available. Do not describe the final clean DOCX as the original annotated source.

## What the numbers mean

The 18-request comparison is a constructed planning illustration, not observed field savings. The current source planner assigned 18 requests with 28.8 km of additional travel and Rp438,700 of scenario service cost; a grouped dedicated collection of the same requests used 112.8 km and Rp662,400. The dedicated scenario exceeded the Rp600,000 budget by Rp62,400. Coverage comparisons using different served totals do not establish cost savings.

The financial case uses explicit team assumptions. The base 2027–2029 projection contains 9,240 received devices; its 29.6–82.7 tonne weight range is estimated incoming device mass, not recovered-material yield or completed recycling. The 9,000 funded management/operations hours are not a headcount of new jobs. Purchased transport/receiving budgets already belong in costs and are not extra profit.

The Monte Carlo run uses 10,000 draws with seed 20261104 and a specified dependency/draw order. Its frequencies are conditional on team-assumed inputs, not measured probabilities of market success. The full model, equations, schedules and scopes remain in V4 and source-current. Do not copy the tariffs, adoption schedule, financial horizon or distributions into a different venture.

## Transfer the workflow, not the project assumptions

Use this case to learn how to connect argument, operating picture, evidence, finances, implementation and a verifiable dossier. For a new project:

- Read its actual guidebook and brief completely.
- Select references for a particular writing/design job.
- State its own problem, mechanism, actor incentives and implementation.
- Define its own units, assumptions, comparisons and evidence scopes.
- Parameterize document/chart settings rather than copying this competition’s five pages, 10 pt appendix permission, logos, QR or decade.
- Render and inspect the actual deliverables, then preserve a new project handover.

The reusable tools and skill are the production entry point for new work. This case’s historical builder may use paths and filenames from the original environment; keep it as source history rather than assuming it is the portable generic engine.
